# 16 — Milestone 2: First Camp

## Milestone Name

```txt
First Camp
```

## Purpose

First Camp is the milestone where Ashenmoon stops feeling like a combat prototype and starts feeling like a survival sandbox.

The player should not merely pick up random items. They should begin to understand:

- water must be found, made safer, and managed
- fire is a survival tool, not just decoration
- materials can be processed into useful things
- the forest has food, danger, shelter, and movement patterns
- a camp is the first anchor point for the player journey

## Core Feeling

The player starts at the bottom but sees a path upward.

Not through a heroic destiny. Not through MMO quest glitter. Through practical survival.

```txt
I have a fire.
I have a place to work.
I can dry materials.
I can make crude tools.
I can mark this place.
The woods are still dangerous, but I am not just prey anymore.
```

## Primary Experience Pillars

### 1. Survival Puzzle

The first problem is simple and physical:

```txt
How do I get water, fire, and safety, then keep them running?
```

The player should need to make decisions around camp placement, fuel, water source proximity, night, weather, and danger.

### 2. Forest Danger

The forest is not a flat item field. Animals live there. Some flee, some graze, some hunt, some threaten, and some attack if hungry or cornered.

The player should understand that staying somewhere too long or moving carelessly can create danger.

### 3. Crafting and Discovery

The player should constantly feel that items might combine, process, react, or reveal new uses.

Crafting should be based on:

- physical processing
- survival problem solving
- material behavior
- experimentation
- learned shortcuts

## First Concrete Objective

The first objective is not an explicit quest log objective. It is an implicit survival objective:

```txt
Make a basic camp where you can cook food, process materials, and craft simple items.
```

The player should arrive at this goal through need:

- thirst pushes them toward water
- cold/night pushes them toward fire
- crafting pressure pushes them toward a work surface
- food/material pressure pushes them toward drying/cooking
- navigation pressure pushes them toward markers/signs

## Required New Content

### Camp Structures

- campfire
- drying rack
- primitive work surface
- crude shelter
- marker/sign

### Environment

- one strong forest biome
- river or pond water sources
- tree concentrations
- bushes and grass
- useful plants
- stones and clay deposits
- animal nests/dens
- feeding zones
- trails/clearings/landmarks

### Creatures

- rabbits
- deer
- boars
- wolves

### Events

- wolf howls nearby
- animals attacking animals
- bird flocks revealing corpse/berries/points of interest
- rain

### Night Pressure

- cold exposure
- need for fire
- poor visibility

## Non-Quest Direction

Ashenmoon should not use explicit MMO-style quests for this milestone.

No:

```txt
Quest Started: Build a Campfire
Objective Complete: Collect 3 Sticks
```

Use implicit pressure instead:

- player gets cold
- darkness falls
- fire becomes useful
- rain threatens fire/materials
- animals behave around food, fire, and presence
- discovered recipes appear after experimentation or observation

## Success Criteria

The milestone is working if a new player can spend 10–20 minutes and naturally experience this arc:

```txt
lost and empty-handed
finds water and materials
learns raw water is risky
makes or uses fire
builds a primitive camp piece
processes at least one material
crafts at least one useful item
sees at least one animal behavior/event
survives or fears the first night
understands camp is the beginning of progression
```

## Design Warning

This milestone should not become a full settlement simulator, advanced NPC system, or ecology research project.

The goal is one playable forest survival loop.

Not everything alive needs a family tree, tax records, and childhood trauma. Yet.
